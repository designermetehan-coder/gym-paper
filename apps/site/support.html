Common issues

  • My steps don't show up: turn on step access in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › GYM Paper.
  • My data doesn't reach my other device: make sure iCloud is on with the same Apple Account on both devices; the first sync can take a few minutes.
  • No notification when rest ends: turn on notifications in Settings › Notifications › GYM Paper.
  • Widget isn't updating: open the app once; widgets refresh within a few minutes. Health data can't be read while the device is locked.
  • I want to delete my data: delete the app; for the iCloud copy go to Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage › GYM Paper.
