FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is GYM Paper really free?

Yes. There's no subscription or in-app purchase, and we don't show ads.

Do I need an account?

No. Download GYM Paper and start right away. Your data is stored on your device and in your own iCloud account.

Can I enter my own program?

Yes. You set the exercises, sets, reps and rest times; supersets are supported. Or start with one of the ready-made programs.

How is my calorie target calculated?

Your basal metabolism and daily needs are calculated from height, weight, age, sex and activity level, with a deficit based on your weekly goal. The target never goes below your basal metabolism. You can also set it manually.

What is written to Apple Health?

With your permission, completed workouts and the weight you enter are saved to Health; steps, active energy, weight and height are read from it. Your Health data isn't sent to servers; the only exception is your daily step count, shared with the people you invite if you turn on Team.

What happens to my data if I change iPhones?

Sign in with the same Apple Account and it arrives on your new phone through iCloud.

What's on Apple Watch?

Start today's workout from your watch, complete sets and manage the rest ring; heart rate and summary on screen. Add fasting, calories left, steps and today's workout complications to your watch face.

Why is it free?

I built GYM Paper for my own training; it isn't a business. So that everyone can use it freely and safely, there are no ads, subscriptions or accounts.

What does Team share?

Team is optional and off by default. If you turn it on, only your display name, your daily step count and whether you worked out that day are shown to the people you add with an invite code. Weight, nutrition and workout details are never shared; stop sharing and your record is deleted from the server.

Is there intermittent fasting tracking?

Yes. Turn it on in Profile; your fasting and eating windows count down live on Today and on your watch face, with optional notifications.
